# Introduction to Artificial Intelligence

## What is AI?

Artificial Intelligence (AI) is a branch of computer science that focuses on creating machines and software that can perform tasks that normally require human intelligence.

These tasks include:

- Learning
- Reasoning
- Problem solving
- Understanding language
- Recognizing images
- Making decisions

## How AI Works

AI systems generally use:

1. Data
2. Algorithms
3. Computing power
4. Models

The system learns patterns from data and uses those patterns to make predictions or decisions.

## Examples of AI

- Chatbots
- Voice assistants
- Face recognition
- Recommendation systems
- Self-driving vehicles
- AI image generators

## Benefits of AI

- Automates repetitive tasks
- Processes large amounts of data
- Can work continuously
- Helps with decision making
- Can improve productivity

## Conclusion

AI allows computers to perform tasks that usually require human intelligence. It is widely used in areas such as healthcare, education, finance, transportation, and entertainment.
